Privacy

What PokeMMO Prospero accesses, and what it does with it. Last updated 7 October 2026.

In short: the title has no servers of its own and collects nothing. Everything it keeps stays on your PS5, or, if you turn on the backup, in a folder of your own Google Drive that only you can reach.

Google Drive backup (optional)

When you sign in with Google from the title, it asks for one permission:

PermissionWhat it allows
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file Seeing, creating, changing and deleting only the files the title itself created in your Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other file, folder or setting in your Google account.

With it, the title keeps a folder called PokeMMO Prospero in your Drive, containing:

FileWhat it isWhy
roms/<file>The game ROM files you put on the console (.nds, .gba) So they can be put back after the title is installed again
profile-<number>.tarOne PS5 profile's PokeMMO settings folder: game options, controls, and PokeMMO's remembered login if you chose "remember me" in the game So each profile's settings and login come back after a reinstall

The number in a profile file's name is the console's internal number for that PS5 profile; it identifies nothing outside your console.

What the title does with it

  • Data goes only between your PS5 and your own Google Drive, directly. It is never sent to the project, its developers or anyone else, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • The title reads these files back only to restore them onto your console when you ask it to.
  • It never deletes your ROM backups. It replaces a profile's settings file with a newer copy when those settings change.
  • The sign-in is kept only on the console, in the title's own storage, to keep the backup running. Installing the title again removes it.
  • Anyone who can open your Google Drive can see this folder, including the settings and remembered PokeMMO logins of every profile backed up from your console. Only turn the backup on with a Google account you trust with that.

PokeMMO Prospero's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Delete the PokeMMO Prospero folder in Google Drive to remove the backup.
  • Remove the title's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the title on the PS5 to remove everything it keeps there.
