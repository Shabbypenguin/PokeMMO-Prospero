PokeMMO Prospero

The official PokeMMO client, running as homebrew on a jailbroken PS5.

PokeMMO Prospero is a free, open-source PS5 homebrew title. It downloads the official, unmodified PokeMMO Linux client from PokeMMO's own server and runs it on the console, with the controller, the touchpad as a mouse, an on-screen keyboard and a TV-friendly setup. You supply the game ROMs you own; the title takes them over a web page or FTP from your phone or computer.

Source and downloads on GitHub Controls

Google Drive backup

Installing the title again empties its storage on the console. So that ROMs and logins aren't lost, the title can keep a copy in your own Google Drive. It is optional, and you turn it on from the console: the TV shows a code, you approve on your phone at google.com/device.

The title uses Google's most limited Drive permission: it can only see and change the files it created itself, in a folder called PokeMMO Prospero. It cannot see anything else in your Drive. What it accesses, and what it does with it.
